# Slide 2: Existing Research & Engineering Gaps

## Industry & Academic Limitations
- **Isolated ML Models**: Traditional ML projects focus solely on raw classification metrics (e.g. basic XGBoost accuracy) without evaluating downstream analyst decision-support value.
- **Data Leakage in Random Splits**: Academic literature frequently evaluates models using random train/test splits, causing severe optimistic bias due to temporal data leakage.
- **Black-Box AI Decisions**: Unexplained ML predictions lack transparency, forcing human analysts to spend significant time manually gathering context.
- **GenAI Hallucination Risk**: Deploying unconstrained LLMs risks hallucinations, unsupported fraud declarations, and illegal autonomous enforcement actions.
