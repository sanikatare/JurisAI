# Slide 1: Problem Statement

## The Financial Fraud Challenge
- **Massive Financial Losses**: Card-not-present (CNP) transaction fraud causes billions of dollars in global annual losses.
- **Extreme Class Imbalance**: Fraud accounts for less than 3.5% of total transaction volume in real-world streams.
- **Evolving Adversary Tactics**: Fraudsters frequently alter device signatures, identity combinations, and transaction velocity to bypass static rule engines.
- **High False Positive Cost**: Legacy rule engines generate overwhelming false positive alerts, burdening human compliance operations and frustrating legitimate customers.
