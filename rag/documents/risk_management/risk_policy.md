# Enterprise Financial Risk Management & Decision Policy

**Document ID**: `POL-RISK-2026-v1`  
**Category**: Risk Management  
**Source**: Enterprise Risk Governance Board  
**Effective Date**: January 2026  

## 1. Operating Principles
1. **Decision Authority**: Automated ML models generate continuous risk scores and calibrated probabilities. Human analysts maintain decision authority for account enforcement actions.
2. **Cost-Sensitive Operating Point**: Decision thresholds are calibrated against operational costs ($15.00$ per false positive investigation vs 100% loss per false negative fraud).
3. **Auditing & Reproducibility**: Model versions, feature schemas, prompt versions, and knowledge base document versions must be logged for every investigation.
